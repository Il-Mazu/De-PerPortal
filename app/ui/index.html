Library & connection

Keep Dè PerPortal and its release files beside Cemu, with your figure dumps in the neighboring NFC folder.

Trap clearing is disabled because it can make trap dumps unreadable. Restore latest trap backup recovers original files saved before a clear; restart Cemu after restoring. Backups are stored in de-perportal-data/trap-backups.

Artwork downloads from the Emulanders community pack and stays on your computer. Skylanders artwork belongs to its respective owners.

Use the supported skylandersNFC/Cemu-Skylanders-Emulated-Portal build. Shortcuts work while Cemu has focus and a Skylanders game is running.
