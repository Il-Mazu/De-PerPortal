# Portal artwork

Skylanders portal designs belong to Activision. These images are not covered by the code's GPL license. This is an unofficial fan companion.

The original `../portal.png` asset is retained for Spyro's Adventure and Giants. Those games share the original stone-ring design. Imaginators uses the arched Swap Force design; it did not introduce a distinct new portal design. Region/platform bundles can vary. References: [Portal of Power overview](https://skylanders.fandom.com/wiki/Portal_of_Power), [Activision's Imaginators portal FAQ](https://support.activision.com/skylanders-imaginators/articles/skylanders-imaginators-portal-of-power-faq).

The following transparent PNGs were AI-adapted on September 18, 2026, with the imagegen skill from product photographs, to align the viewpoint, remove cables/backgrounds and fit the GUI. They are illustrative adaptations, not official Activision product renders.
