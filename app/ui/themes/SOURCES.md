# Theme assets

## Dè FishBet

`fishbet-logo.png` was supplied by the maintainer; `fishbet-fish.png` is the mascot cut out of it.

## Dè Cell to Singularity

`singularity-logo.png` was supplied by the maintainer. The theme parodies the idle game [Cell to Singularity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_to_Singularity) by Computer Lunch: its Tech Tree of Life, deep-space look and Entropy/Ideas currencies. No artwork from the game is bundled.

## Dè perThumpback

Built around [Thumpback](https://skylanders.fandom.com/wiki/Thumpback), the Water Giant: his catchphrase, his Phantom Tide backstory and his Skylanders: Giants stats. The banner shows Thumpback's portrait from the artwork you downloaded or chose in Settings; nothing of his is bundled. It uses `textures/deck.jpg`, `rope.jpg` and `net.png` below, plus `parchment.jpg` for the ship's log.

## Dè PerMCdonald

A parody of a fast-food self-order kiosk, in McDonald's red (`#DA291C`) and yellow (`#FFC72C`). No McDonald's artwork, logo file or typeface is bundled: the arches in the header are a simple SVG drawn for this theme, and Poppins stands in for the brand's own Speedee typeface.

## Dè perDoomScroll

A parody of vertical-video feeds (TikTok, Reels, Shorts): TikTok's cyan `#25F4EE` and pink `#FE2C55`, reels with like rails, For You tabs and a screen time counter. All likes, views and comments are made up. No app artwork is bundled; icons are system emoji.

## Game themes

The six game themes (`games.css`) take their cues from each game: Eon's book and the Core of Light (Spyro's Adventure), the Arkeyans and the orange Giants logo (Giants), Woodburrow and the top/bottom swap (Swap Force), Cloudcracker Prison and Traptanium (Trap Team), vehicles, racing and Rifts (SuperChargers), Mind Magic and Imaginite (Imaginators). No Activision artwork was added for them.
